Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Overview

LaneRelay is a Chrome extension designed to help users work faster inside DAT by creating broker emails, managing templates, saving notes, setting reminders, and saving preferred brokers and lanes.

Information LaneRelay May Use

LaneRelay may use visible load-board information needed to provide its features, such as origin, destination, broker name, rate, lane details, email fields, saved templates, notes, reminders, and user settings.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide LaneRelay functionality, including email templates, auto-filled outreach, notes, reminders, saved preferences, and extension settings.

Data Selling

LaneRelay does not sell user data.

Data Sharing

LaneRelay does not share user data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Storage

Settings, templates, notes, reminders, and preferences may be stored locally in the browser or through Chrome extension storage so the extension can remember the user’s preferences.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@lanerelay.com
