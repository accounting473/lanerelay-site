Privacy Policy

LaneRelay Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Overview

LaneRelay is designed to help users work faster inside supported load board pages by generating broker outreach, hiding duplicate loads, and helping perform broker safety checks.

Data Collection

LaneRelay should only access page information needed for its core features. This may include visible load details, broker/contact details, lane information, and user-provided template settings.

Data Use

Information is used to generate emails, improve workflow, manage duplicate-load display, and support broker checks. LaneRelay should not sell user data.

Third-Party Services

If FMCSA or other public broker-check sources are used, LaneRelay may open or request public information from those sources to help users review broker details.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@lanerelay.com
