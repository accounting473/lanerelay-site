LaneRelay Broker outreach for load boards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

1. Overview

LaneRelay ("we," "our," "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that helps dispatchers and owner-operators work faster on freight load boards by providing one-click broker email, templates, notes & reminders, saved preferred lanes, and supporting tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information LaneRelay accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

LaneRelay is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any third-party load board provider.

2. Information We Access

2.1 Data You Provide

You may voluntarily provide the following inside the extension's Settings tab:

  • Your name, company name, phone number, MC# and DOT# (used in email templates and signatures)
  • A "from" email address (optional; defaults to your Gmail address)
  • A company logo image (optional; embedded in your outgoing emails)
  • A custom HTML signature (optional)
  • Email templates you create or edit
  • Notes and reminders you add
  • Preferred lanes / loads you save

2.2 Data From Load Board Pages

When you have a supported load board page open, LaneRelay reads publicly visible load information from that page in order to provide its features. This includes:

  • Origin and destination cities/states
  • Pickup dates, rates, mileage, weight, equipment type
  • Broker company name, broker email addresses (visible on the board), MC#, phone number

This information is read directly from the page you have already loaded in your own browser. LaneRelay does not crawl, scrape, or access load board servers directly.

2.3 Google Account Data

To send emails on your behalf, LaneRelay uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization with the gmail.send scope. When you click "Authorize Gmail," Google's authorization screen will ask for your consent.

With your consent, LaneRelay receives a Gmail access token that allows it to:

  • Send emails on your behalf to broker addresses you select

LaneRelay does NOT access, read, modify, or store any of the following:

  • Your Gmail inbox or any incoming email
  • Your contacts
  • Your drafts, sent items, or any other Gmail folders
  • Your Google account profile beyond what's needed for authentication

Limited Use Disclosure (required by Google): LaneRelay's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use Information

All information accessed by LaneRelay is used solely to provide extension functionality, specifically:

  • Filling email templates with the correct load and contact details
  • Sending those emails through your authenticated Gmail account, to broker addresses you click
  • Saving your notes, reminders, preferred lanes, and settings locally so they persist across sessions
  • Showing reminders / notifications at times you have scheduled
  • Marking already-emailed broker contacts to prevent duplicates

4. Where Information Is Stored

All settings, templates, notes, reminders, saved preferred lanes, and your Google OAuth token are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API.

LaneRelay does not operate any external servers or databases. Your data does not leave your browser except in the following cases:

  • When you click an email pill, the email is sent through Google's Gmail API directly from your browser to Google's servers (this is required to deliver the email).
  • When you click the in-app Maps button, the destination is opened in Google Maps in a new tab.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for advertising, marketing, or any other commercial purpose. We do not use your data to train any model or build any aggregate dataset.

The only third parties involved are Google (for Gmail sending and account authentication) and the load board you are using (which you are already a logged-in user of). LaneRelay does not transmit your data to any other party.

6. Permissions Explained

LaneRelay requests the following Chrome extension permissions:

  • storage — to save your settings, templates, notes, etc. on your local device.
  • identity — to authenticate with your Google account via OAuth so emails can be sent on your behalf.
  • alarms — to schedule reminders you set on your notes.
  • notifications — to display system notifications when your reminders fire.
  • host permissions on supported load board domains — so the extension can run inside the load board pages and offer its features.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally in your browser, you control its lifetime:

  • To clear settings, templates, notes, or preferred lanes: use the relevant "delete" or "clear" buttons inside the extension's tabs.
  • To remove the Google OAuth token: open chrome://settings/connectedApps (or visit myaccount.google.com/permissions) and revoke LaneRelay's access.
  • To remove all data at once: uninstall the extension from chrome://extensions. This deletes all locally stored data.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All communication with Google's Gmail API uses HTTPS.
  • OAuth tokens are stored using Chrome's secure extension storage and only accessible to LaneRelay itself.
  • LaneRelay does not transmit your data anywhere except as described above.

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

LaneRelay is intended for use by professional dispatchers and motor carriers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of LaneRelay after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
